# APA-CARRIER rev A -- carrier PCB for the Auto Polar Alignment base

Generated by `generate.py` (single reproducible script, see the end of this file). Authoritative design: `SPEC.md`.
**KiCad itself was NOT available: the `.kicad_pcb` / `.kicad_sch` were written by this script and parsed back with kiutils only.
KiCad ERC and DRC have NOT been run and are still outstanding.** Pours are stored as zone outlines; press B (fill all zones) in KiCad.

Board: 85 x 60 mm, 1.6 mm FR4, 2 layers, 2 mm corner radius, four M3 NPTH holes (3.2 mm).
Tallest component: 17.1 mm above the top face (18.7 mm above the bottom face): the plugged-in TMC2209 module with heatsink (assumed envelope). Next tallest: PTC 14 mm, C1 12 mm, C2/C3 11 mm, screw terminals 10 mm (all assumed typical). The J1 socket protrudes 8.5 mm below the board.

## Spec problems found (SPEC.md was not silently changed)

1. **TMC2209 socket row spacing (mechanical, was a real error)**: SPEC.md said 15.24 mm, so a real module would not plug in; the real Pololu/SilentStepStick pin rows are 12.70 mm apart (15.24 x 20.32 mm is the module *size*). The lead has since corrected SPEC.md; the generator default is now 12.70 mm (`--driver-row-spacing`).
2. **Via vs. annular-ring rule**: vias 0.6/0.3 mm have a 0.15 mm ring while "plated through-hole annular ring >= 0.3 mm" is also stated. I applied 0.3 mm to component holes and 0.15 mm to vias (fab capability not checked).
3. **PTC voltage rating**: the TVS (1.5KE18A) clamps near 25 V, so choose PTCs rated for at least 24-30 V; many 16 V radial PTCs do not have a 5.08 mm lead pitch (not verified against a specific part).
4. 3V3 track width not specified (0.5 mm used). With D1 in series the motor rail is about 0.4-0.5 V below the PD voltage (not an error).

## Placement decisions and assumptions

- **Pi stack.** Pi Zero 2 W geometry is from the official mechanical drawing RP-008358-DS (read from the PDF's vector data): 65 x 30 mm, mounting holes 3.5 mm from the edges, 40-pin header centred on the 65 mm edge with its two rows on the hole centreline (3.5 mm from the top edge), pin 1 = square pad at the left end of the inner row. J1 is centred on the carrier at x = 42.418 mm, y = 12.446 mm (12.4 mm from the south long edge); the Pi hangs below J1 and its body sticks 14.1 mm out of the south edge, so the USB and mini-HDMI connectors (bottom long edge of the Pi) stay uncovered. The carrier is 20 mm wider than the Pi (10 mm each side); the SD slot (left short edge) and CSI connector (right short edge) are reached from the sides, 11 mm below the carrier underside (check this against your enclosure). The silkscreen shows the Pi top edge, its two short edges (clipped at the board edge) and the two Pi mounting holes; the full 65 x 30 outline is in `carrier_outline.dxf` layer `PI_OUTLINE`. This is my reading of the SPEC sentence "the board overhangs the Pi so USB/HDMI/camera connectors stay uncovered"; the spec text "J1 along one long edge" is met loosely (J1 sits in the southern 20 % of the board, the south zone holds the end-stop connectors and filters that use the odd J1 pins).
- **Why J1 is not on the very edge.** The odd (south) row of J1 carries 10 signals. On a 2-layer board they can only leave through the 0.94 mm gaps between the 1.6 mm pads (0.3 mm track, 0.32 mm clearance) or around the ends of the header. The three AZ step/dir/enable signals use hand-placed waypoints through those gaps (`PREROUTE` in the script); everything else is autorouted.
- **TMC2209 modules.** Rows 12.7 mm apart (the lead corrected SPEC.md from 15.24 mm; Pololu A4988 drawing: board 15.2 x 20.3 mm, rows 12.70 mm apart). The BIGTREETECH TMC2209 is *assumed* to share the Pololu / SilentStepStick footprint: **unverified** for the V1.3 board; its pin order is from the generic BTT wiki page (global.bttwiki.com/TMC2209.html), also **not confirmed for V1.3**. Module bodies U1/U2 are 9.5 mm apart (>= 1 mm required) and no tall part is within 1 mm of a module. Module envelope assumed 15.24 x 20.32 mm, top at 17.1 mm above the socket seat (heatsink included).
- **Footprints** follow the KiCad official library (GitLab `kicad-footprints`, read at build time): JST XH B2B/B4B-XH-A (pads 1.7 x 1.95, drill 0.95, body 5.75 deep), DO-201AD (pad 3.2, drill 1.6, P12.70 variant), Phoenix MKDS-1,5-2-5.08 terminal (pad 2.6, drill 1.3, 10.2 x 9.8), radial electrolytics (pad 1.6, drill 0.8). The 2.54 mm header pads are 1.6 mm (drill 1.0, ring exactly 0.30 mm). **Unverified:** the PTC envelope (10 x 4 x 14 mm assumed), electrolytic and terminal heights (12/11/10 mm typical), JST height 7 mm, socket body heights 8.5 mm. Screw-terminal wire entry is assumed to face the nearest board edge.
- **Track widths.** 0.8 mm for 12 V, motor coil and 5 V nets (about 2 A at 10 K rise, IPC-2221); 0.5 mm for 3V3 (SPEC gives no width for it); 0.3 mm for signals. Vias 0.6 / 0.3 mm. GND is a pour on both layers with 0.3 mm thermal gaps, 0.5 mm spokes and 133 stitching vias on a 5 mm grid.
- **R2, R3, C8, C9** (0805, 0.6 mm tall) sit in the pocket between the two socket rows, under the plugged-in module (8.5 mm of headroom). **J11/J12 labels** share one label column between the two headers. These silk texts could not be placed without overlapping pads or other silk and were omitted (J7..J10 per-pin SIG/GND labels are among them; some J1/module pin labels too): +12V, 5V_PI, ALT MOTOR, AZ MOTOR, EN, ES1-4 = AZ MIN, AZ MAX, ALT MIN, ALT MAX, GND, MS1, RXD, SAL, SIG, VIO. Pin 1 of every header is a square pad; the connectors' pin 2 is GND.

## Regenerating

```
python3 -m venv env && env/bin/pip install kiutils gerbonara ezdxf shapely numpy scipy matplotlib trimesh manifold3d mapbox-earcut pillow
env/bin/python generate.py                       # default driver row spacing 12.70 mm
env/bin/python generate.py --driver-row-spacing 15.24 --out /some/dir   # spec's old value: still routes, but reports a J6/D2 courtyard overlap, so the north-side placement would need re-spacing
```
Run time is about 30 s on a laptop (routing is a pure-Python A*; two consecutive runs give byte-identical Gerbers, KiCad files and netlist). The only non-deterministic input is the run date printed on the silkscreen.

## Cautions (added by the lead after the software review)

- J11/J12 pin 3 carries 5V_F. Power I2C breakouts from 3V3 (pin 2) unless they are known to level-shift: a 5 V pull-up on SDA/SCL can destroy the Pi's GPIO.
- Check the TMC2209 module's pin 4 (PDN_UART versus DIAG/INDEX) against its drawing before plugging both modules in.
- End stops are wired common + normally-closed (see SPEC.md): a pressed or broken switch reads HIGH.
